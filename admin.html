Upload sales & stock (CSV or Excel)

Sales report: upload the shop's sales report workbook as it is (e.g. 20261019_WB_SP_Sales_Report.xlsx). Each sheet named like "Wiper Blade" or "Spark Plug" with columns Date, Product, Quantity, Unit Price (GHS), Sales Amount (GHS), Discount (GHS) is read; Product is the packaging (Loose, Brown box, White box, White plastic, Blue box, Bundle; Box for spark plugs), Quantity is in packs and is converted to pieces, and revenue is Sales Amount minus Discount. Other sheets (e.g. Summary) are skipped. The report is a running list, so re-uploading it replaces that product's report rows for the dates it covers — nothing is counted twice.

Template (for deliveries, adjustments, transfers, or sales with customers): Excel (.xlsx) or CSV with columns (header row required, any order): date, sku, variation, type, qty, unit_price, customer, note, order_ref. These rows are added, not replaced.
date YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY · sku U01 or U02 · variation e.g. FG1 (can be blank when the product has only one) · type sale, receipt, adjustment or transfer · qty whole pieces (sale/receipt positive; adjustment/transfer signed, e.g. −20) · unit_price GHS per piece for sales (blank = retail price) · order_ref groups lines into one order for discount tiers. A file with any bad row is rejected as a whole.
